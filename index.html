Privacy Policy

App: Tasbeeh Counter - Zikr & Dhikr

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Tasbeeh Counter - Zikr & Dhikr is designed as a standalone, distraction-free utility application to assist users with their daily dhikr, tasbeeh, and spiritual supplications. We believe in minimal permissions and zero invasive tracking.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our application operates locally on your mobile device:

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identity details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or photos.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register, or log in to use this app.
  • Local Storage Only: All counter values, prayer counts, custom target settings, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device's local memory (offline storage). This data never leaves your device.

3. Device Permissions

To provide a tactile and responsive counting experience, the app may request basic device hardware access:

  • Vibration (Haptics): Used exclusively to deliver subtle tactile feedback when you increment, complete, or reset a counter. This permission does not access private files or personal records.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The application does not use third-party analytics platforms, behavioral trackers, or intrusive advertising SDKs that harvest user activity or device identifiers.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not target, solicit, or collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the application complies with child online privacy regulations.
